## Fixed selection

Reconstruct all 864 historical views: two sizes × three batch tags × three actual bit/key caps × three FPR ceilings × two rounds × eight contracts. The four query metrics are `lookup_ns`, `hash_lookup_ns`, `inline_lookup_ns`, `inline_hash_lookup_ns`; construction metrics are `insert_ns`, `allocate_insert_ns`, `hash_insert_ns`, `allocate_hash_insert_ns`. Contracts and rounds are never pooled or replaced by a cross-contract minimum.

In each view, consider every Poppy geometry for that size. Eligibility requires actual bucket-plus-cache bytes ≤ n × cap / 8 and the formal historical two-sided95% Wilson upper endpoint ≤ the view's ceiling. Retain **every exact median tie** for the minimum eligible Poppy latency. This is a within-Poppy shortlist even when all Poppy points lose against other libraries. A view with no eligible Poppy point stays explicit and contributes no substitute. The union across all views determines the proposed bridge constructions; its size is unknown before selector execution and cannot exceed47.

Record all 20,304 Poppy view/geometry alternatives, including over-budget points, failed historical quality gates and slower eligible points. There are 282 Poppy process rows, 2,256 old metric observations and 47 unpooled formal quality records. Repeated views, batches and rounds add no calibration denominator. Retain the source row/case/round/filter position, historical count/denominator and quality gate, actual allocation, metric and tie membership. Independently compare every reconstructed Poppy leader set against the completed analysis's recorded `poppy_external` leader sets.

## Output schemas

- `views`: all864 fixed identities, eligible count, minimum historical latency or null, every tied configuration ID, and explicit no-eligible status.
- `alternatives`: all20,304 size-matched geometry/view records with `fits_cap`, historical `quality_pass`, `eligible`, `selected_in_view`, historical metric/source-row identity and failure reasons.
- `quality`: all47 formal Poppy quality records and their three historical ceiling outcomes; never source the earlier constructor-audit FP counts.
- `catalog`: every47 original canonical constructor and source aliases, whether selected, and every view that selected it. Preserve real n, exact f64 FPP bit pattern, public None/Space/cache policy, bucket count/capacity, probe count, cache bytes, bucket bytes and total backing.
- `union`: selected canonical constructions with all supporting view IDs and exact immutable source identities.
- `future_public_forms`: exactly two entries per selected construction: original raw-u64 little-endian byte8 public membership and natural typed-u64 public membership. Both require new focused allocator/semantic proof and new quality/performance observations. Their future mapping IDs are distinct; historical qualification/latency is not copied into a future qualification field.
- `provenance`: exact raw/analysis/scope/bridge/source inventory hashes and the complete producer audit result. No running-raw8 path is an input.

The typed-u64 mapping has not been measured by the old Poppy panel. Consequently this union is a finite historically motivated shortlist, not evidence that every omitted geometry will remain slower under natural byte8 or typed-u64 inputs. Preserve the complete47 catalog, all historical failures and the source catalog's over-cap exclusions. Any decision to expand beyond the selected union requires a separate fixed predata coverage decision.

## Load and guard plan

Verify committed source/import inventories before importing the immutable producer audit. Verify all77 raw files, the raw manifest and complete launcher log, all17 listed completed-analysis artifacts plus their manifest and the additive scope archive. Reuse the producer's complete36-process/486-row command/raw/geometry/quality audit before selection. Reconstruct the Poppy views directly from those validated rows and formal quality records, then compare to the archived analysis leader sets. No previously measured median is converted into a new raw-contract cost.

The scope note is mandatory: old whole-input caller scratch was allocated before clocks and could remain256MiB at the large size; its allocation+hash+insert metric is not the newer raw8 complete-build metric. Old later construction repetitions checked only `min(n,batch)` inserted keys. The new bridge keeps required scratch allocation in the complete-build interval, zero scratch for both natural Poppy APIs, all-key/no-FN validation, public Result handling and poisoned dense outputs. Source rules, not old observations, govern the future caller.

Synthetic guards will cover exact full coverage, null views, ties, smaller allocations under larger caps, historical quality failure versus byte-budget failure, per-round/per-contract winners, constructor/alias identity, zero imported future qualification, and rejection of incomplete or altered committed sources. Actual completed-data selection executes once only after source review/freeze and a coordinated offline lease; no BF or compiler is invoked.
