# Interpretation and retained losses

This completed160-row study strengthens the original **public PImpl** comparison under one GCC12.2/Boost1.83 raw-u32 caller. It does not replace the separate modern-library, direct/static public-template or Rust comparisons. Query repeats share one constructed bitmap per process, and the two launch rounds use the same seen seed42; neither is a fresh independent accuracy sample.

The best eligible project control wins all42 views with an eligible PImpl comparator. The remaining30 views are **not wins**:24 correspond to cap12 at0.1%/0.01%, where neither side qualifies; six are large cap24 at0.01%, where Range/K12 qualify but no PImpl does. At cap12 none of the four production controls qualifies even for1%; those project wins therefore belong to optional Range or experimental K12. Production, optional and experiment identities remain separate in every stored ranking.

Nearby geometry improves the observed FPR at all12 unique public points, reproducing the earlier accuracy-only pilot exactly. Its speed is not uniformly better: among168 unconditional nearby/original mode pairs there are76 wins and92 losses. Of84 same-condition pairs across the two launch rounds,20 win both rounds,28 lose both and36 change direction. These are descriptive observed medians, not significance or an odd-divisor-only causal claim. U1/U2/U4 remain separate timed public shapes but do not create independent quality samples.
